Privacy

Breaking Bread is a Bible study app for a men's group. This says exactly what it

stores, who can read it, and what leaves your phone.

Last updated 11 September 2026.

What it stores

Your account. An email address, and a display name you choose. There is no

password — signing in is a link emailed to you.

What you write. Notes, highlights, bookmarks, prayer requests, messages in a

study's discussion, and the studies you create. All of it is kept on our

Supabase database until you delete it or delete your account.

Which studies you are in, and whether you have marked a week's reading as

done.

Your device, if you turn notifications on: the notification token Apple

gives us, and whether the app was built for testing or release. Nothing else

about the device.

What it does not store

  • No password. There isn't one to lose.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or health data. The app never asks.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. There is no third-party

analytics library in the app, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

  • No scripture reading history. Which chapters you open is not recorded

anywhere. Only what you deliberately mark or write is kept.

Who can read what

This is enforced by the database itself, not by the app being careful.

Who can read it
Your notesOnly you, unless you share one to a study
A note shared to a studyThe men in that study
Your highlights and bookmarksOnly you. There is no way to share them
Your foldersOnly you, even when a note inside is shared
Prayer requestsThe men in that study
Anonymous prayer requestsThe men in that study see the request. Nobody but you can see it was yours — the author is withheld at the database, not hidden by the app
Discussion messagesThe men in that study
Your name and bioMen who share a study with you
Your notification tokenNobody. Only our server reads it, to send you a notification

Men in different studies cannot see each other's anything.

Camera and microphone

Used only during a live study, and only while you are in the room.

The leader's camera and microphone are used so the group can see and hear the

teaching. Everyone else has a microphone available and **cannot turn a camera

on at all** — the permission to publish video is granted per person by our

server and refused for everyone but the leader.

Nothing is recorded. Live video and audio pass through LiveKit

(livekit.com) to the men in the room and are not stored by us or by them.

Where it lives

  • Your data is stored by Supabase (supabase.com) in the United States.
  • Notifications go through Apple's Push Notification service. Apple sees

the notification text, which may include the name of a study or the first

words of a message.

  • Live video passes through LiveKit's servers while a session is running.
  • The Bible text is inside the app and never leaves your phone. Reading it

needs no connection and sends nothing anywhere.

Deleting your account

In the app: Me → Delete my account. It removes your profile, your notes,

highlights, bookmarks, folders, prayer requests, messages, and any study you

started that nobody else joined.

If you own a study other men are in, the app will ask you to hand it over or

delete it first, so a group's discussion does not disappear with you.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Children

The app is for adults in a church men's group. It is not directed at children

and collects nothing knowingly from them.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Asking

Questions about any of this go to the person who runs your study group.
